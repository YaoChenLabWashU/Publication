PURPOSE
-------
Computes and plots transition-triggered FLiP lifetime (zeroed, not Z-scored)
for two groups in dorsal CA1: FLIM-mAKAR (membrane-targeted experimental
sensor) and PKI (protein kinase A inhibitor control). Runs nested statistics
comparing the two groups across all transition types. Produces Figure 1f
for the manuscript.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESSING PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Raw .mat files (both groups)
  |
  +--> choose_excluded_acqs()
  |      first_acqs = 3; pull_baseline = True from Excel.
  |
  +--> build_classes()   [called separately per group]
  |      Constructs FLiPExperiment objects.
  |      microarousals = True; zscore = False; raw_lifetime = False
  |      |
  |      +--> FLiPExperiment()  [called internally]
  |             Loads .mat file, extracts FLiP lifetime, scores sleep states.
  |
  +--> transition_triggered_lifetime()   [called per group]
  |      Aligns zeroed FLiP lifetime to sleep state transitions.
  |      Transitions: NREM-Wake, REM-Wake, Microarousals, Wake-NREM, NREM-REM
  |      experimental_sensor = 'FLIM-mAKAR'
  |      window = [50, 150] s; NREM_cutoff = 100 s; wake_cutoff = 100 s
  |      zscore = False; raw_lifetime = False
  |      Returns: fig_dict, lifetime_dict (per group)
  |
  +--> transition_triggered_quant()   [per group, per transition type]
  |      Quantifies amplitude in specified post-transition windows.
  |      NREM-Wake: [50,75]; REM-Wake: [50,75]; MA: [10,30]
  |      Wake-NREM: [75,100]; NREM-REM: [75,100]
  |
  +--> nested_analysis_stats_v2()   [FLIM-mAKAR vs PKI, per transition]
         Stage 1: robust HuberT per-animal means.
         Stage 2: sign permutation test (primary) + Welch t-test (secondary).
         MA result: p = 0.003
