PURPOSE
-------
Fits a Cox frailty proportional hazards model (executed in R) to predict
time-to-wake from NREM sleep using three predictors: PKA-SP (z-scored FLiP
lifetime), microarousal count, and ZT time. Prepares the data in Python,
calls an external R script, and loads/visualizes bootstrap and permutation
results including cumulative microarousal effects and shuffled control
comparisons. Produces Figures 5e, 5f and Extended Data Figures 5d–f for
the manuscript.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESSING PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Raw .mat files
  |
  +--> choose_excluded_acqs()
  |      first_acqs = 3; pull_baseline = True from Excel.
  |
  +--> build_classes()
  |      sleep_states = True; microarousals = False (MAs defined manually)
  |      |
  |      +--> FLiPExperiment()  [called internally]
  |             Loads .mat, extracts FLiP lifetime, sleep states.
  |
  +--> PKA.define_MAs()
  |      Identifies microarousal bouts: MA threshold m = 16 s.
  |      Returns MA onset/offset arrays per acquisition.
  |
  +--> FLP_exp.ss_onset_offset()
  |      Extracts NREM bout onset/offset times.
  |
  +--> PKA.find_continuous()
  |      Identifies contiguous NREM segments for further processing.
  |
  +--> PKA.shuffle_singlestate()
  |      Generates per-experiment shuffled FLiP lifetime (chunk shuffled).
  |      Z-score computed within each experiment.
  |
  +--> PKA.hazard_model()   [primary analysis]
  |      Prepares survival data table (event = wake, time = NREM duration,
  |      predictors = PKA-SP, MA count, ZT category).
  |      Exports .csv to each experiment folder.
  |      Calls R: final_frailty_model_withMAZT_logtime.R via subprocess.
  |      Returns: hazard ratio table loaded from R output .csv.
  |
  +--> [Load bootstrap/permutation results]
  |      natsort.natsorted(glob.glob(HazardModel/*.npy)) per experiment
  |      Loads pre-computed bootstrap HR distributions and permutation HRs.
  |
  +--> [Cumulative MA analysis]
         Plots how PKA-SP hazard ratio changes as MA threshold m increases.
         Loads pre-computed results from HazardModel/ folders.

Model results at m = 16 s:

  MA:      HR = 0.7617,                  p = 2.73e-58
  MA attenuation of PKA-SP HR: 34.4%
