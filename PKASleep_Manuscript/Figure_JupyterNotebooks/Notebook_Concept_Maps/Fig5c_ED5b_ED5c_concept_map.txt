PURPOSE
-------
Plots Delta EEG power trajectories during NREM bouts, binned by bout length,
for all 24 mPFC FLIM-mAKAR experiments. Tests whether Delta power increases
or decreases with NREM bout length using Wilcoxon signed-rank tests and
Mann-Whitney U tests. Produces Figure 5c, Extended Data Figure 5b, and
Extended Data Figure 5c for the manuscript.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESSING PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Raw .mat files
  |
  +--> choose_excluded_acqs()
  |      first_acqs = 3; pull_baseline = True from Excel.
  |
  +--> build_classes()
  |      sleep_states = True; microarousals = True
  |      |
  |      +--> FLiPExperiment()  [called internally]
  |             Loads .mat, extracts FLiP lifetime, sleep states.
  |
  +--> PKA.transition_triggered_lifetime()
  |      NREM-Wake transitions only; window = [300, 30] s
  |      zscore = True; just_dictionary = True (returns raw dict, no plot)
  |
  +--> PKA.transition_triggered_power()
  |      Aligns Delta EEG power to NREM-Wake transition.
  |      freq_band = Delta [0.5, 4] Hz; EEG channel = 0
  |      window_length = 10 s; noverlap = 9 s
  |
  +--> [Binning by NREM bout length]
  |      bin edges = np.arange(50, 300, 50) → 4 bins: 50-100, 100-150, 150-200, 200-250 s
  |
  +--> PKA.plot_binned_power_p2()
  |      Plots mean ± SEM Delta power traces per bin.
  |      norm_val = mean of all Delta power across all bouts
  |
  +--> scipy.stats.wilcoxon()
  |      One-sample, alt='less': tests if normalized power < 1 in late NREM.
  |
  +--> scipy.stats.mannwhitneyu()
         Tests for difference in peak Delta power between short and long bouts.
