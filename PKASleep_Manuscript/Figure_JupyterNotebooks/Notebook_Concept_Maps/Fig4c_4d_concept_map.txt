PURPOSE
-------
Fits a two-state exponential model to FLiP lifetime from a single mPFC
FLIM-mAKAR experiment (ltFLiPAKARmemEEGEMG0072, mouse 4610) using BIC
optimization. Performs a coarse random parameter search followed by local
L-BFGS-B refinement, parallelized with joblib. Plots 1D BIC profiles across
each parameter to characterize the landscape. Produces Figures 4c and 4d
for the manuscript.
