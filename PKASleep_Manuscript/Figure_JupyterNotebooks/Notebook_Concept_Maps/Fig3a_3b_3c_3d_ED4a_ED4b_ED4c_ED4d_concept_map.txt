PURPOSE
-------
Computes mutual information between EEG band power and FLiP lifetime across
four frequency bands (Delta, Theta, Sigma, Beta) for the 24-experiment mPFC
FLIM-mAKAR dataset. Plots normalized cross-correlation / mutual information
time courses, tests peak timing against zero with Wilcoxon signed-rank tests,
and compares experimental to shuffled data. Produces Figure 3a–d and Extended
Data Figure 4a–d for the manuscript.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROCESSING PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Raw .mat files
  |
  +--> choose_excluded_acqs()
  |      first_acqs = 3; pull_baseline = True from Excel.
  |
  +--> build_classes()
  |      sleep_states = False; shuffle_window = 100
  |      |
  |      +--> FLiPExperiment()  [called internally]
  |             Loads .mat file, extracts FLiP lifetime.
  |
  +--> [loop per experiment, per EEG channel (0, 2), per band]
  |
  +--> SWS_utils.bandPower()
  |      Computes band power time series from EEG.
  |      window = 10 s; noverlap = 9 s (1 s steps)
  |      freq_dict: Delta[0.5,4], Theta[4,8], Sigma[10,15], Beta[15,30]
  |
  +--> PKA.interpolate_photometry()
  |      Resamples FLiP lifetime to match band power timebase.
  |
  +--> PKA.nlcc_mi()
  |      Computes normalized lag cross-correlation and mutual information
  |      between band power and FLiP lifetime.
  |      max_lag = 250 s (250 samples); num_bins = 100
  |
  +--> Per-animal averaging
  |      Averages MI time courses across recordings from same animal.
  |
  +--> [across all animals]
  |      Mean ± SEM; plot window = [-100, 100] s
  |
  +--> scipy.stats.wilcoxon()
         One-sample Wilcoxon on peak lag values vs zero.
         All bands / both channels: p < 0.001
